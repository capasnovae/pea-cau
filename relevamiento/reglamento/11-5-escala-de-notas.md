### 11.5 ESCALA DE NOTAS

| EXAMEN ► | TRABAJO PRÁCTICO |

| 9 | 90 a 94% |

| 7 | 76 a 80% |
| 6 | 70 a 75% |
| 5 | 60 a 69% |
| 4 | 50 a 59% |
| 3 | 30 a 49% |
| 2 | 20 a 29% |
| 1 | 0 a 19% |
